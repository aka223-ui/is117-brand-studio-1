    Free guide / no signup

    Good gift. No lore exam.

    You do not need to know every episode to choose thoughtfully. Start with what your person loves.

    • Check the show. Ask whether Evangelion is one of their favorites; anime fans have different tastes.
    • Check the artwork. Show them the graphic, or ask whether they prefer bold mecha designs or something understated.
    • Check the size. Confirm their preferred size. Demo sizes are S–XL; garment measurements and fit information are not supplied.
    • Check before buying. This is an unofficial student prototype, not a store. For an actual purchase, check the seller’s product details, delivery dates, and return terms.

    Keep this checklist handy for a future gift. No account or email needed.

    Our starting point / shared enthusiasm

    From your ride.
    To your rotation.

    Ita-Tees takes its name from itasha, the practice of decorating cars with anime graphics. We bring that spirit of visible self-expression to a white tee concept: wear the art you love and let your interests start a conversation.
