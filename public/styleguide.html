06 / Voice

Words are
design, too.

Creator voice: bold, playful, welcoming. Speak to fans and gift buyers without invented claims.

Playful and helpful

“Good gift. No lore exam.”

A free checklist welcomes gift buyers who do not know the show.

Bold and welcoming

“Wear your fandom out loud.” Unity connects itasha and apparel as shared fan expression. Reciprocity offers a useful gift checklist freely, with no signup. No invented community size, reviews, urgency or endorsement.

A living reference. Change the shared tokens and logo, then compare this board with the storefront at the same width. Check the logo, typography, components, color roles, and voice text; sharing a stylesheet alone does not keep every example current. Save the reason for your choices in SPEC.
