Why this direction?

Design reference: Martine Bedin, Super lamp prototype (1981), made by Fausto Celati for Memphis, V&A.

The reference combines a blue semicircular body, wheels and differently colored exposed bulb holders, making a familiar lamp look like a playful toy. Here, a tilted sticker-like product frame quotes decorated cars; italic editorial lettering contrasts with a technical grid and monospace labels. Purple and lime recur across these mixed visual languages. The aim is energetic, expressive fandom without changing the product or message.

Risk: Layering can compete with the shirt artwork; gift buyers may find the tone less straightforward.
