Why this direction?

Design reference: Josef Müller-Brockmann, Musica Viva (1958), MoMA.

The reference uses a large diamond field, small colored squares and carefully grouped type. Here, geometric spacing and a two-column grid give the shirt and message distinct jobs. A single sans-serif family creates a readable hierarchy; purple appears as a restrained signal. These choices aim to let fans inspect the artwork and gift buyers understand the concept quickly.

Risk: The controlled layout could feel too reserved for fans seeking bold self-expression.
